Toolkit 01 · Patient Care Framework

PCF (People-Centred Framework) Knowledge Hub

Updated 2026 WHO Co-Design
Download Full Toolkit (ZIP) ↓ Related Course →

Developed in collaboration with WHO and national TB programmes, the People-Centred Framework aligns national strategic planning with the continuum of patient care, from community awareness and care-seeking to diagnostic confirmation and treatment completion.

  • Patient Pathway Analysis (PPA) & Cascade-of-Care Diagnostic Tool
  • Root-Cause Barrier Assessment for Missing Persons with TB (FTMP)
  • National Strategic Plan (NSP) Prioritization Matrix & Costing Templates
  • District-Level Quarterly Programme Review Facilitation Guide

Toolkit 03 · Non-Invasive Diagnostics

Stool-based Testing for TB (Simple One-Step Method)

WHO-Endorsed SOS Method Paediatric TB
Download Full Toolkit (ZIP) ↓ Related Course →

Pioneered by KNCV TB Plus laboratory specialists, the Simple One-Step (SOS) stool processing method enables rapid molecular diagnosis of paediatric TB using standard Xpert MTB/RIF Ultra cartridges without requiring invasive gastric aspiration or complex laboratory equipment.

  • Laboratory Bench-Top SOP: Simple One-Step (SOS) Stool Processing
  • Paediatric Clinical Algorithm for Non-Invasive Specimen Collection
  • Caregiver Illustrated Instruction Leaflet for Stool Sample Collection
  • External Quality Assessment (EQA) & Biosafety Checklist
