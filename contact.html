How do I enrol in a course?
Select any course in the Course Catalogue, click “Enroll Now”, and sign in or create a free learner account on the KNCV Learning Management System (LMS).
Are the courses free?
Yes. The majority of KNCV Knowledge Exchange self-paced courses, webinars, and implementation toolkits are open-access and free of charge for healthcare professionals and public-health practitioners worldwide.
Will I receive a certificate?
Yes. Upon completing all required modules and passing the course assessment, you can download a verifiable KNCV TB Plus Certificate of Completion directly from your LMS profile.
I forgot my password: what do I do?
Open the KNCV Learning Management System via “Learner dashboard ↗” in the site footer (or click “Enroll Now” on any course page) and select “Forgot password?” on the LMS login screen to receive an automated password reset link via email.
Can my organisation request tailored training?
Yes. KNCV TB Plus works closely with Ministries of Health, National TB Programmes, and implementing partners to deliver tailored cohorts and blended country trainings. Contact us via email at info@kncvtbc.org.
Which languages are courses available in?
Courses and toolkits are primarily offered in English, with core clinical and programmatic courses also available in French, Spanish, and Russian depending on regional programme needs.
