Drug-resistant TB Management

Practical clinical and programmatic training for physicians, nurses, and National TB Programme teams on diagnosing RR/MDR-TB and implementing shorter all-oral BPaLM/BPaL regimens safely and effectively.

Duration: 12 hours
Curriculum: 4 modules
Format: Self-paced
Languages: English, French
Rating: 4.9 (320 reviews)

About this course

Developed by KNCV TB Plus clinical specialists and international partners, this course equips healthcare professionals with the latest evidence-based protocols for managing rifampicin-resistant and multidrug-resistant tuberculosis (RR/MDR-TB). Learners explore rapid molecular drug-susceptibility testing, eligibility criteria for the 6-month BPaLM and BPaL regimens, active drug-safety monitoring and management (aDSM), and people-centred adherence support.

What you will learn

  • Interpret rapid molecular drug-susceptibility results (Xpert MTB/XDR, line probe assays, and targeted NGS)
  • Select appropriate all-oral shorter regimens (6-month BPaLM/BPaL and 9-month regimens) based on patient profiles
  • Monitor and manage adverse events including peripheral neuropathy, myelosuppression, and QT prolongation
  • Apply clinical guidance for special populations including pregnant patients, children, and people living with HIV
  • Implement active TB drug-safety monitoring and management (aDSM) within facility workflows
  • Integrate people-centred counselling and Digital Adherence Technologies (DAT) to support treatment completion
